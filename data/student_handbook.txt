STUDENT HANDBOOK: CAMPUS LIFE, CONDUCT AND POLICIES
====================================================

Section 1: Campus Decorum, Identification and Timings
1.1 College Hours and Identity Cards
The regular instructional hours of the college are from 8:30 AM to 4:45 PM, Monday through Friday. Every student must wear the official RFID Student Identity Card prominently around the neck at all times while inside the academic campus, laboratories, and library. Security staff are authorized to deny entry to any student without a valid ID card.

Key Provisions:
  * Campus Gates: In-time cutoff is 8:45 AM. Latecomers require HOD clearance after 3 infractions.
  * Central Library Hours: Open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays; 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on Saturdays.
  * Hostel Timings: Hostel gates close strictly at 9:00 PM for all resident students.
  * Vehicle Regulations: Students riding two-wheelers must wear helmets. Speed limit on campus is 20 km/h.

Section 2: Anti-Ragging Statutory Guidelines
2.1 Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Mandate
Ragging in any form is strictly banned inside and outside the college campus as per UGC Regulations 2009 and Supreme Court of India directives. Ragging is defined as any disorderly conduct whether by words spoken or written, or by an act which has the effect of teasing, treating or handling with rudeness any student.

Key Provisions:
  * Punitive Actions: Immediate suspension, FIR registration with Police, debarment from scholarship, cancellation of admission, and expulsion from hostel.
  * National Anti-Ragging Toll-Free Helpline: 1800-180-5522.
  * Institutional Anti-Ragging Squad Phone: +91-94401-22334 (Available 24x7).
  * Affidavit: Every student and parent must submit an online anti-ragging undertaking at www.antiragging.in.

Section 3: Library Services, Book Borrowing and E-Resources
3.1 Central Library Circulation Guidelines
The Central Library houses over 85,000 volumes, 120 national and international journals, and digital IEEE/ACM subscriptions. Undergraduate students are entitled to borrow 4 books simultaneously for a duration of 14 days.

Key Provisions:
  * Overdue Fine: INR 2 per day per book for the first week, and INR 5 per day thereafter.
  * Reference Books: Dictionaries, encyclopedias, and single-copy volumes cannot be taken out of the reading room.
  * Digital Library: 60 high-speed multimedia workstations with access to NPTEL, IEEE Xplore, and DELNET.
